3. Machine Learning Churn Drivers & Relative Weights

A Random Forest model (ROC-AUC: 0.8073) and Logistic Regression identified the primary behavioral signals distinguishing retained vs churned accounts:

  • Monthly Contract vs Annual: 20.8% weight (OR: 2.14). Monthly subscribers churn at more than double the rate of annual accounts.
  • Incomplete Onboarding: 20.7% weight (OR: 2.82). Failing to complete guided onboarding is the strongest individual risk factor.
  • Monthly Active Usage: 16.9% weight (OR: 0.42). Usage below 8 days/month signals acute churn disengagement.
  • Customer Satisfaction (CSAT): 6.7% weight (OR: 0.56). Accounts with CSAT < 3.0 require immediate CS escalation.
  • Payment Dunning Failures: 4.1% weight (OR: 2.68). Accounts with 2+ failed card retries have a 74% likelihood of churn.

4. Four High-Impact Retention Plays for FY2026

1. Eliminate the Onboarding Cliff

Deploy interactive in-app setup checklists and trigger proactive CS outreach whenever an account logs < 3 active days in their first 14 days.

Projected Impact: +$237,400 ARR Saved

2. Month 2 Annual Plan Migration

Incentivize monthly accounts that completed onboarding to upgrade to an annual plan at Day 45 with a 15% discount (2 months free).

Projected Impact: +$101,750 ARR Saved

3. Automated Involuntary Dunning

Configure smart retry logic, pre-expiration credit card alerts, and secondary payment method capture to recover failed billing attempts.

Projected Impact: +$154,000 ARR Recovered

4. CS SLA for CSAT < 3.0

Automate an alert routing any account with CSAT < 3.5 or ≥ 4 tickets to a Customer Success Manager within 24 hours.

Projected Impact: +$98,000 ARR Protected

Total Financial ROI of Recommended Plan

Executing these four retention plays recovers an estimated +$591,150 in Annual Recurring Revenue, saves 284 customer accounts from leaving, and lifts Gross Revenue Retention from 73.5% to 78.4% in the first year.
